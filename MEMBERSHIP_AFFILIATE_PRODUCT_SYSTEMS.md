# Membership, Affiliate, and Product Systems

This document defines how reusable educational systems, content systems, and product systems can support memberships, affiliate programs, and distribution models inside the wider `lippytm` ecosystem.

## Purpose
Turn strong learning systems, workflow kits, and content systems into repeatable offerings that can be reused, adapted, distributed, and maintained.

## Core Principle
A reusable system should be able to support:
- members
- affiliates
- content distribution
- product sales
- ongoing updates
- adaptation for different audiences

## System Types

### 1. Membership Systems
Use for:
- tiered learning paths
- member-only lesson kits
- recurring update systems
- community builder tracks

Focus:
- continuity
- update cycles
- reusable member resources

### 2. Affiliate Systems
Use for:
- partner onboarding kits
- reusable campaign support
- content reuse paths
- derivative landing and lesson packs

Focus:
- consistency
- repeatable promotion support
- reusable partner assets

### 3. Product Systems
Use for:
- starter kits for sale
- educational bundles
- workflow packs
- chatbot, AI, and blockchain practice packs
- support and update add-ons

Focus:
- modular packaging
- clear setup and support notes
- reusable product structure

### 4. Content Systems
Use for:
- educational articles
- short lessons
- walkthrough packs
- public demos
- distribution through multiple channels

Focus:
- repurposing content across audiences
- connecting content back to learning systems and products

## Best Practices
- keep the base product or system modular
- document update and maintenance expectations clearly
- keep member and affiliate variations tied to a stable core
- include setup, support, review, and security notes
- convert strong products into reusable templates when possible
