# Pack to Product Transition Model

This document defines how reusable study packs, toolkit packs, sandbox packs, and specialization packs can transition into stronger productized deliverables inside the wider `lippytm` ecosystem.

## Purpose
Help the ecosystem move strong reusable learning and support assets into clearer product forms without losing setup clarity, review discipline, or maintainability.

## Core Principle
A reusable pack should become a product only when it is clear, supportable, reviewable, and useful beyond a single local context.

## Transition Stages

### 1. Pack Stage
Use for:
- reusable study materials
- reusable setup materials
- reusable sandbox structures
- reusable specialization materials

### 2. Product Candidate Stage
Use for:
- packs with repeated value
- packs with clearer support notes
- packs that reduce repeated confusion
- packs that can be used by more than one audience

### 3. Product Stage
Use for:
- supportable deliverables
- member-facing materials
- affiliate-ready materials
- releaseable educational products

## Product Readiness Signals
Promote when:
- setup notes are clear
- review and security notes are attached
- repeated use shows the pack is durable
- support expectations are visible
- weaker overlapping versions can be simplified or archived

## Best Practices
- keep the product form simpler than the local variants it came from
- attach support and refresh expectations clearly
- connect product transitions back to standards and health checks
- retire weak overlapping versions when stronger products emerge
- use productization to reduce confusion, not add more clutter
