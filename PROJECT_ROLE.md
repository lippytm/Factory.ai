# Project Role

## Purpose
`Factory.ai` is the productization and build-factory hub for the wider lippytm ecosystem.

## Primary Objectives
- turn ideas into reusable kits, templates, and repeatable systems
- support productized AI, automation, and educational assets
- document build recipes and deployment patterns
- connect experiments and learning projects to real deliverables
- support commercialization without collapsing creative diversity

## Inputs
- concepts from R&D repos
- templates and workflows
- educational assets
- product ideas
- automation patterns

## Outputs
- reusable kits
- starter packs
- product templates
- deployment recipes
- repeatable service and build patterns

## Connected Repositories
- `lippytm-lippytm.ai-tower-control-ai`
- `lippytm.ai`
- `MyClaw.lippytm.AI-`
- `Web3AI`

## KPIs
- reusability of outputs
- clarity of build systems
- number of repeatable templates produced
- usefulness for productization and delivery
