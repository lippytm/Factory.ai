# P-011-EEBDS-001 — Encyclopedia Content Factory Specification

**Canonical parent:** `lippytm/Prompt-11-`  
**Factory role:** Reusable workflow and bot-template specifications for Character–Ecosystem Innovation Units  
**Status:** Architecture specification v0.3; proposed workflows are not represented as deployed production services  

## Purpose

Factory.ai shall provide reusable, testable workflow specifications for transforming approved Prompt #11 inputs into Encyclopedia educational entertainment and Business of Businesses deliverables.

The factory does not decide factual truth, rights ownership, legal compliance, medical validity, certification, publication, minting, or final commercial release. It coordinates bounded tasks and produces evidence for qualified reviewers and HumanApprovalGate.

## Input contract

Every production run requires an Encyclopedia Entry Passport containing:

- CEIU or entry ID;
- title and version;
- privacy class;
- character and Character Passport references;
- ecosystems and Life/Business Sciences domains;
- truth labels;
- Quantum Questions;
- learning objectives;
- story premise;
- innovation and Build Mode requirements;
- business hypothesis;
- requested media;
- model-line assignment;
- quality level and RiskGate;
- human approval status and scope.

The factory must reject or quarantine work packets with missing identifiers, invalid privacy routing, incompatible approval scope, or Red RiskGate.

## Production pipeline

### Stage 1 — Intake and validation

- validate the Entry Passport;
- verify canonical module and schema version;
- confirm work-packet owner and destination;
- enforce privacy class;
- check approval scope;
- create immutable run ID and audit event.

### Stage 2 — Source and truth mapping

- register sources and dates;
- map factual claims to sources;
- assign truth labels;
- identify contradictions and unresolved questions;
- mark date-sensitive claims for refresh;
- block unsupported material claims.

### Stage 3 — Character and fiction boundary

- verify Character Passport;
- separate creator, brand, AI interface, avatar, and fictional persona;
- confirm voice, likeness, image, and performance permissions;
- record extraordinary abilities as fictional;
- check originality and third-party rights risks;
- preserve limitations, costs, and consent boundaries.

### Stage 4 — Ecosystem mapping

Map applicable effects across:

- natural;
- human;
- social-political;
- knowledge;
- digital-cyber;
- business-economic;
- cosmic-speculative environments.

### Stage 5 — Quantum Questions generation

Produce bounded work products for:

- IF: evidence, assumptions, scenarios, consequences, success criteria;
- MAYBE: alternatives, uncertainty, contradictions, unresolved questions;
- WHY NOT: opportunities, prototypes, reforms, and business experiments;
- DON'T DO THAT: prohibited actions, abuse cases, stop-work triggers, and safe alternatives.

### Stage 6 — Curriculum design

- define audience and prerequisites;
- convert objectives into measurable outcomes;
- create exercises and assessments;
- specify vocabulary and facilitator guidance;
- plan accessibility alternatives;
- identify expert review requirements.

### Stage 7 — Story and worldbuilding

- produce an original premise, conflict, stakes, and character arc;
- map story events to learning objectives;
- preserve fact-versus-fiction markers;
- balance power with limitations and consequences;
- define humor and audience boundaries;
- avoid copied third-party lore and designs.

### Stage 8 — Build Mode design

- define a safe prototype or exercise;
- create requirements and test plan;
- define security, privacy, accessibility, environmental, and rollback reviews;
- limit the build to defensive, educational, or constructive uses;
- prohibit harmful intrusion, sabotage, evasion, coercion, weaponization, and unsafe medical activity.

### Stage 9 — Business of Businesses design

- document customer or community problem;
- define value proposition and offer;
- test pricing assumptions;
- identify support and operating requirements;
- select lawful revenue pathways;
- define affiliate and licensing rules;
- include no-guarantee disclosures;
- evaluate accessibility, externalities, and environmental impact.

### Stage 10 — Media adaptation

Generate medium-specific production plans for approved formats:

- ebook;
- audiobook;
- video book;
- interactive video;
- documentary;
- fictional film or animation;
- comic;
- music film or trailer;
- podcast;
- course;
- workbook;
- game or simulation;
- chatbot or character tutor;
- code repository;
- toolkit;
- franchise kit.

Every adaptation must preserve material context, truth labels, fictional disclosures, rights, limitations, accessibility, corrections, and product identity.

### Stage 11 — Independent model-line production

Maintain isolated namespaces for:

- ChatGPT Business;
- Gemini/NotebookLM;
- Claude/Fabric Hermes.

Each line produces its own source manifest, claim map, creative treatment, contradiction report, deliverables, and Quality Evidence Packet.

Comparison and merged-premium stages remain locked until all required independent lines are approved.

### Stage 12 — Quality assembly

Assemble:

- requirements ledger;
- test evidence;
- defect register;
- RiskGate decision;
- release-gate matrix;
- rights and license evidence;
- privacy and security evidence;
- accessibility and environmental evidence;
- media manifests;
- NFT-safe metadata draft;
- correction, supersession, retirement, and archive plans.

### Stage 13 — Human review

The factory may route the Quality Evidence Packet to authorized reviewers. It cannot approve itself.

HumanApprovalGate remains mandatory for publication, commercial launch, minting, identity cloning, voice or likeness use, legal or medical claims, financial actions, and franchise certification.

### Stage 14 — Packaging and delivery handoff

After approval, package outputs for the designated repository or channel. The package must contain:

- product and version IDs;
- approved files;
- manifests and checksums;
- approval scope;
- required disclosures;
- accessibility assets;
- correction and withdrawal procedure;
- monitoring requirements.

### Stage 15 — Monitoring, correction, and retirement

- collect learning, quality, safety, accessibility, environmental, customer, affiliate, and revenue metrics;
- register defects and incidents;
- propagate corrections;
- quarantine unsafe versions;
- supersede or retire products;
- preserve archival records.

## Work-packet controls

Each work packet shall include:

- packet ID;
- run ID;
- source CEIU and version;
- assigned role;
- permitted input classes;
- permitted output destination;
- model line;
- task description;
- prohibited actions;
- required outputs;
- expiration;
- reviewer;
- audit reference.

Default routing is one task to one authorized destination. Broadcast requires explicit authorization and must never include confidential or restricted data.

## Stop-work triggers

- invalid or missing Entry Passport;
- privacy class incompatible with destination;
- unsupported material claim;
- missing Character Passport or rights permission;
- copied third-party content with unresolved rights risk;
- unsafe build requirement;
- prohibited real-world intrusion, sabotage, evasion, coercion, weaponization, or medical action;
- guaranteed income, funding, investment, health, legal, or environmental claim;
- inaccessible critical user journey;
- material unmitigated environmental risk;
- failed critical gate;
- Red RiskGate;
- missing, expired, or revoked human approval.
