## Factory boundary

Factory may create scaffolds, tests, manifests, database migrations, diagnostic rules, and reports. It may not deploy to production, publish, charge customers, transfer funds, sign contracts, mint NFTs, broaden permissions, or certify itself.

A swarm remains a sandbox or pilot until its tests, privacy route, security review, cost limit, and human decision are recorded.
