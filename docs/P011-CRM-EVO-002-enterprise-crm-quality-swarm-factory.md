## Swarm Blueprint

Every generated swarm must declare:

- swarm and Agent Passport IDs;
- human owner and purpose;
- permitted inputs, privacy classes, providers, tools, outputs, and destinations;
- prohibited actions;
- budget, timeout, concurrency, and expiration;
- evaluation suite, thresholds, red-team tests, and regression tests;
- audit events, correction, rollback, shutdown, revocation, and retirement;
- HumanApproval requirements.

## Manufacturing lifecycle

`request → validate → scaffold → unit test → privacy/security review → sandbox → red-team → evidence assembly → human review → approve, reject, quarantine, or retire`

Factory may generate code, schemas, tests, plans, manifests, and reports. It may not import or contact real people, change consent, merge identities, make high-impact decisions, deploy to production, publish, charge, contract, transfer funds, mint NFTs, certify itself, or grant HumanApprovalGate.

## Continuous improvement

Repeated telemetry may produce a reviewable evolution proposal. No proposal may rewrite the factory or activate itself. All changes remain versioned, reversible, and independently reviewable.

This document defines manufacturing architecture, not an already deployed autonomous swarm factory.
